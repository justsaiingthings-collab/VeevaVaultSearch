VaultSearch V2: The AI-Safe Evolution

VaultSearch V1 ships without AI.
Here's exactly why — and what comes next.

V1 is deterministic by design. In regulated environments, reproducibility and audit trails are non-negotiable. We're building V2 to add AI capabilities incrementally, only where they can be made safe, explainable, and still fully auditable.

Planned Features

The V2 roadmap.

Each feature solves a specific pain we've heard from QA teams. Each one includes a technical approach that keeps safety and explainability front-and-center.

Natural Language → VQL Compiler
In Design
The Problem
The rule-based parser handles ~80% of queries well. But compound or ambiguous requests fall through: "SOPs modified after the last deviation for Batch 7734" or "deviations that triggered a CAPA still open."
The Approach
Fine-tuned LLM trained on your Vault's schema and valid VQL patterns. All generated VQL is shown to the user before execution and logged. Human-in-the-loop: user reviews and approves the query.
Why Not V1?
Required a curated VQL training corpus scoped to your Vault's schema. We're building that library now with early customers.
Semantic Document Similarity
Planned Q3 2026
The Problem
Two SOPs that cover the exact same process have wildly different names. Native search misses the relationship. You'd have to know both names to find both documents.
The Approach
Embedding-based similarity run on document metadata (titles, descriptions, keywords) — not content, to stay within Vault data residency rules. Similar documents surfaced as "Related" cards in the result panel.
Why Not V1?
Embeddings require a vector store outside Vault. Data residency and compliance implications need legal sign-off with your IT and security teams.
Anomaly Detection in Deviation Trends
Research
The Problem
A spike in deviations for a product line goes unnoticed until the audit. No one is actively watching the pattern — they're only reacting to individual records as they come in.
The Approach
Time-series analysis on deviation open/close rate by product, site, and severity. Weekly digest surfaced in the search panel for QA Admin role. Threshold-based alerts.
Why Not V1?
Requires historical data aggregation and stable patterns. Piloting with 3 customers first to validate utility before general release.
Predictive CAPA Closure Risk
Research
The Problem
CAPAs go overdue because no one flagged early that this particular CAPA was likely to slip based on the team's historical closure pattern and severity.
The Approach
Lightweight classification model trained on historical CAPA metadata (severity, assigned team, product line, days-to-close). Risk score on open CAPAs. Feature weights always shown — no black box.
Why Not V1?
Model requires 12+ months of CAPA history per tenant to be reliable. Cold-start problem. Building with customers who have the data depth.
Cross-Document Knowledge Graph
Planned Q4 2026
The Problem
Finding all documents related to a deviation (the SOP it violated, the CAPA it spawned, the training records it triggered) requires 5 separate searches today.
The Approach
Build a relationship graph from Vault object references and custom links. One-click "Show all related documents" per result. Relationship types displayed (violates, triggers, supersedes, etc.).
Why Not V1?
Graph construction requires custom Vault SDK work and metadata standardization across object types. Scoping the work now.
Smart Query Suggestions from Usage Patterns
Planned Q3 2026
The Problem
Suggested chips are role-based and static. A QA manager who runs "High Severity Deviations" every Monday should see that at the top automatically. Personalization is missing.
The Approach
Frequency-based personalization per user, purely local (no cross-user data or training). Purely sessionStorage + optional server-side persistence (if opted in).
Why Not V1?
Requires usage tracking infrastructure and privacy controls. Building the architecture now.

Trust by Design

What we won't do.

These commitments are permanent, not marketing. They reflect what regulated environments require.

🔐
We won't use AI for access control.
ACL decisions stay deterministic. AI surfaces documents; humans make the access decision. Vault's native permissions always enforce the boundary.
📤
We won't send content to external LLMs without explicit opt-in.
Document text stays in your instance. If we offer an optional feature that requires external APIs, you'll see it, agree to it, and control it.
📋
We won't replace VQL in the audit log with "AI decided this."
Every search is logged with human-readable VQL, template name, user, timestamp. Inspectors can read it. No black boxes in the audit trail.
💡
Every AI action has a human-readable explanation.
Risk scores show feature weights. Generated queries show the VQL before execution. Anomalies link to the data they're based on. No opaque recommendations.
